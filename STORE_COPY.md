# Sprout, the plant buddy

**Hook:** A tiny circuit-board sprout that cries a little blue tear when your plant is thirsty.

**Description (60 words):**
Sprout is a real circuit board that lives in your plant pot. Its stem senses soil moisture without
touching it, and when the soil dries out a blue LED tear drips under its eye. Boop the bud between its
gold leaves for an instant answer. One coin cell lasts about two whole years. No app, no charging, no dead
plants.

**Suggested price:** $19
